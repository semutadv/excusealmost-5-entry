The excuse that almost works, in nineteen words, written for the
make.fun bounty of that name: the text a gamer sends the boss to skip
work during GTA 6 launch week. A grandmother's parrot has learned to
say "GTA launch week", and legally — the texter has checked — that is
a family emergency in three states.

## Running it

`node main.ts` clears the output directory's contents and writes the
excuse to `/output/entry.txt` as plain UTF-8 text.
